{# Settings → Reset mirror (routes.factory_reset): everything of the owner's gone, as when the card was flashed. Typing RESET is the server's check; hx-confirm only says what it means. #}

Reset Mirror

Makes the mirror as it was when its card was flashed, for giving it away or starting over: your settings, the admin password, modules and their data, backups, the Wi-Fi networks and the API token are deleted. The mirror restarts and asks for Wi-Fi and a password again, on its own screen.
